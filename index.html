THE RISK

One bad spreadsheet can create expensive marketplace mistakes.

Supplier feeds change constantly. A misplaced decimal, incorrect mapping or sudden stock jump can become a catalog-wide problem if it reaches Seller Central unchecked.

01

Wrong SKU mappings

Supplier identifiers can point to the wrong Amazon listing.

02

Unexpected price jumps

Large increases or crashes can hide in thousands of routine rows.

03

Quantity mistakes

Stock wipes and implausible increases deserve a closer look.

04

Malformed data

Missing products and damaged identifiers can silently break updates.

FAQ

Clear answers before you start.

FeedGuard is designed as a review layer, not an automatic marketplace connection.

Does FeedGuard upload directly to Amazon?

No. The current version generates a reviewed Amazon-compatible file that the seller uploads manually to Seller Central.

Does FeedGuard change my prices automatically?

No. FeedGuard analyzes the feed and flags potentially unsafe changes for review.

Which files are supported?

CSV and XLSX supplier files are supported.

What happens to blocked rows?

Blocked rows are excluded from production export by default.

Do I need an Amazon API connection?

No, not for the current version.

How will paid subscriptions be managed?

Paid subscriptions are planned to be managed through our billing provider's customer portal once paid plans launch.
